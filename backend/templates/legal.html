Privacy, Terms and Cookies

Privacy

We collect your name, email, barangay, optional phone and the reports, photos and map locations you submit, only to route reports to barangay officials and send in-app updates. Under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173) you may access, correct or request deletion of your data and withdraw consent through the barangay data protection officer. Your email and phone are visible only to officials. Reports flagged as suspicious activity show you as Anonymous to other residents.

Terms

Submit truthful reports, do not post unlawful content or other people’s personal data, and do not misuse the Emergency category. This service does not replace 911 or the barangay hotline.

Cookies

Only essential cookies are used (sign-in session and security token), plus your light/dark preference saved on this device. No advertising or tracking cookies.
